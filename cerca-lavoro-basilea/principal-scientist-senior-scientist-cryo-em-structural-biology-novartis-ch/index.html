What if your expertise in structural biology could reveal the molecular insights that unlock the next generation of medicines? As Principal Scientist/Senior Scientist, Cryogenic Electron Microscopy (Cryo-EM) Structural Biology, you will join the Protein Sciences group within Discovery Sciences, applying cutting-edge Cryo-EM to challenging targets across early drug discovery. Working with scientists across Biomedical Research, you will prepare biological samples, generate and interpret high-quality structural data, and translate molecular insights into actionable science that supports the discovery of novel therapeutic candidates.

Job Description

Key Responsibilities

  • Contribute experimentally and scientifically to early drug-discovery projects within multidisciplinary teams.
  • Prepare biological samples for Cryo-EM, including data collection, processing, structural determination, and model building.
  • Apply current Cryo-EM technologies and methodologies to generate high-quality structural insights for drug discovery.
  • Collaborate closely with laboratory associates, providing mentoring and technical training within a matrix environment.
  • Maintain accurate experimental records, scientific documentation, and instrument logs to support data quality and reproducibility.
  • Communicate structural biology findings through internal and external presentations and scientific publications.

Essential Requirements

  • PhD in a relevant scientific discipline with at least five years of research experience, or an equivalent combination of training and experience.
  • Deep expertise in Cryo-EM, including sample preparation, single-particle analysis, structure determination, model building, and structural interpretation.
  • Hands-on experience operating high-end cryo-transmission electron microscopes.
  • Demonstrated experience in the expression and purification of a range of protein targets.
  • Excellent written and spoken English, with strong scientific communication and presentation skills.
  • Strong analytical skills, attention to detail, and a collaborative approach to working within multidisciplinary scientific teams.

Desirable Requirements

  • Experience troubleshooting and optimizing experimental and data-processing workflows for single-particle analysis and cryo-electron tomography.
  • Familiarity with artificial intelligence-based and computational tools for protein construct design and optimization.
  • Experience characterizing protein-ligand interactions.
  • Hands-on experience with single-particle analysis, cryo-electron tomography, and dual-beam scanning electron microscopy.

Why Novartis

At Novartis, you will join a collaborative Protein Sciences team applying complementary technologies, including biophysics, Nuclear Magnetic Resonance spectroscopy, X-ray crystallography, and Cryo-Electron Microscopy, to advance target-based drug discovery and novel therapeutic candidates.
