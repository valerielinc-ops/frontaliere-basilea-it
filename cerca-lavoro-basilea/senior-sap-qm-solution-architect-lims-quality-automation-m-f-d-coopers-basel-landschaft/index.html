For our client in the Basel region, we are looking for an experienced, motivated, and open-minded Senior SAP QM Solution Architect (LIMS & Quality Automation) .

As part of a strategic digital transformation initiative, you will play a key role in shaping the future of quality assurance.

In this position, you will design and develop innovative end-to-end processes for the automated handling of laboratory testing and quality certificates within the SAP environment.

  • For our client in the Basel region, we are looking for an experienced, motivated, and open-minded Senior SAP QM Solution Architect (LIMS & Quality Automation) .
  • As part of a strategic digital transformation initiative, you will play a key role in shaping the future of quality assurance.
  • Your responsibilities:
  • Analyze existing quality management and laboratory processes and define future-state processes for automated inspection lot and certificate handling
  • Several years of experience as a Senior SAP QM Consultant , Solution Architect or in a similar role with strong SAP QM expertise

Responsabilità principali

  • Your responsibilities:
  • Analyze existing quality management and laboratory processes and define future-state processes for automated inspection lot and certificate handling
  • Develop a comprehensive business and IT blueprint for the integration of SAP QM with Laboratory Information Management Systems (LIMS) and external testing laboratories
  • Design automated processes for the receipt, validation and assignment of digital quality certificates (Certificates of Analysis / CoA) to SAP inspection lots
  • Create functional and technical specifications for interfaces based on APIs, EDI, IDocs and Web Services
  • Prepare and support the implementation phase, including SAP QM customizing, development support, integration testing and user acceptance testing
  • Plan and facilitate workshops with business stakeholders, laboratory partners and IT teams in an international environment
  • Advise project leadership and key stakeholders on solution architecture, process design and SAP QM best practices
  • Ensure a sustainable, scalable and future-proof solution architecture for digital quality assurance

Requisiti principali

  • Several years of experience as a Senior SAP QM Consultant , Solution Architect or in a similar role with strong SAP QM expertise
  • In-depth knowledge of quality inspection processes , inspection lot management , quality certificates and usage decision processes
  • Proven experience in the design and implementation of integrations between SAP QM and LIMS systems or external testing laboratories
  • Hands-on experience with the automation of quality certificates (CoA) , including receipt , validation and automated processing within SAP
  • Strong track record in developing business concepts , IT concepts , blueprints and requirements specifications in SAP environments
  • Excellent analytical and conceptual skills combined with a structured and solution-oriented approach
  • Experience in workshop facilitation , stakeholder management and cross-functional collaboration
  • Fluent English language skills , both written and spoken, German is an advantage IT - ERP & Applications Basel-Landschaft Contracting Full Time
